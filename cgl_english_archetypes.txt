SSC CGL TIER-1 ENGLISH — QUESTION-GENERATION MODEL (archetype catalog, v1)
Source: 2,200 English PYQs (2023-2025, 88 shifts). Ranked by observed frequency.
Each archetype = one reusable template SSC repeats: trigger, micro-concept,
standard + fast routes, option tactics, trap, prerequisite, time, verbatim example.
Difficulty/Language/Recognition/Time are heuristic estimates, not measurements.
Error-focus labels (SVA/tense/article/prep/pronoun) are heuristic cues from the
sentence text, not answer-key-derived truth.
