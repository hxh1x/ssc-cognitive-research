==============================================================================
SSC CGL TIER-1 GENERAL AWARENESS: FACT-TAXONOMY MODEL (v1)
Source: 2,200 GA PYQs (2023-2025, 88 shifts).
GA is NOT forced into Quant-style archetypes. Each entry follows:
Domain -> Topic -> Subtopic -> Fact_Type -> Question_Form -> Entity -> Relationship -> Distractor.
Frequencies are measured counts. Entity recurrence below lists only entities
measured 4+ times. Difficulty/Time are heuristic estimates, not measurements.
Static vs Current = explicit year-mention (2019-2026) in the stem.
Known limits: keyword taxonomy (boundary fuzz at domain edges, e.g. temple-
architecture vs religious history); scheme-vs-ministry attribution follows the
stem focus; Hindi-contaminated rows are unrecoverable; assertion rows with bare
stems are classified from option text and marked via their form.
