==============================================================================
SSC CGL TIER-1 REASONING: QUESTION-GENERATION MODEL (mechanism catalog, v1)
Source: 2,200 Reasoning PYQs (2023-2025, 88 shifts). Ranked by observed frequency.
Each archetype names the actual reasoning mechanism: trigger, rule/structure,
standard + fast routes, option tactics, trap, prerequisite, time, verbatim example.
Series mechanisms (AP/gap/alternating/shift/xk+-c) are computed from the numbers
or letter-clusters in the question text. Difficulty/Time are heuristic estimates.
Reasoning Misc holds cross-section contamination (arithmetic/algebra/probability),
situational-judgement items, Hindi-contaminated rows and parser rejects.
==============================================================================
